# GACHA PLANET localization guide (from SEO/localization research, 2026-11-07)

Site: Japanese gacha (capsule toy) info site. Readers: (a) tourists from the region visiting Japan, (b) fans of Japanese characters. Prices are ¥200–600 per play, machines take ¥100 coins only.

## Languages
- zh-cn: Simplified Chinese (mainland). Address reader as 你. Friendly travel-guide tone; sentence-final 哦/啦 sparingly. Currency: ALWAYS write 日元 (never bare ¥ alone in prose; "¥300" in price badges is OK but nearby text must say 日元). Title separator " - " (Baidu dislikes 【】 and emoji in titles). Title ≤ 30 full-width chars ideally; description ≤ 78 chars.
- zh-tw: Traditional Chinese for BOTH Taiwan and Hong Kong. Neutral written Chinese, no Cantonese (no 嘅/係/喺), avoid Taiwan-only words (捷運→地鐵/電車, 超商→便利商店) and HK-only words (推介→推薦, 的士). Friendly, 你, 喔/吧 moderately. Currency 日圓. Price idiom "一轉300日圓". Title separator "｜". Never make political statements; say 台灣・香港的朋友 / 華語圈旅客 if needed.
- ko: Korean. 해요체 for UI/body (friendly), 합니다체 for disclaimers/legal/official FAQ answers. Currency 엔 (e.g. 300엔). Title separator " | ". Main term 가챠 (also naturally mention 캡슐토이, 가샤폰, 뽑기 in body text once in a while).
